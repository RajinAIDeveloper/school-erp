A combined result weights several exams into one, such as an annual result made of 30% half-yearly and 70% final. Only published exams are used. Each subject's percentage is the weighted average of the exams the student sat it in; an absence counts as nought, an exemption leaves that exam out. The class's own rules then grade it, as for a single exam.
