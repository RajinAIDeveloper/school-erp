Online payments

Families pay from their fees page through the school's own SSLCommerz account (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, cards and banks). Money goes from SSLCommerz to the school's bank under the school's contract with them; this system never holds it. A payment is recorded only when SSLCommerz confirms it, and lands in the "Online Payments Clearing" account until it settles. Start with the sandbox, and switch it off only with live store details. The demonstration gateway moves no money and is for showing the system.
