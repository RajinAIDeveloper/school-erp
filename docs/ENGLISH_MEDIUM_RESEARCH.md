# English-medium schools: programmes, rules and market

Research of 23 September 2026 for the owner's primary market: English-medium schools in
Bangladesh following Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel and the International Baccalaureate.

Facts are sourced below. Where a source could not be opened, or only a secondary source was
found, the entry says so; treat those as unverified.

## Programmes and how they grade

### Cambridge

- **Grade scales.** IGCSE is graded A*–G and O Level A*–E, with U for ungraded. A separate set of
  IGCSE (9–1) syllabuses exists in some administrative zones only.
- **AS and A Level.** AS is graded a–e, in lower case. A Level is graded A*–E, and **A* exists only
  for the full syllabus**, never for a single component.
- **Official thresholds are set per exam series**, per option, and per time-zone variant. A
  school's internal exams therefore use the school's own thresholds.
- **Worked example: IGCSE Physics 0625, June 2025.** The components are:
  - Paper 2, multiple choice: 40 raw marks, 30%.
  - Paper 4, theory: 80 raw marks, 50%.
  - Paper 6, alternative to practical: 40 raw marks, 20%.

  They are scaled to a total out of 200. Option CY thresholds were A* 153, A 128, B 103 and C 78.
  **Core papers cap the grade at C–G.**
- **Forecast grades.** Centres submit them; the deadline for the June 2026 series was 30 April.
- **Candidate entries** need:
  - the name exactly as it should appear on the certificate, up to 60 characters;
  - date of birth as dd/mm/yyyy;
  - a 4-digit candidate number;
  - a UCI (unique candidate identifier);
  - syllabus and option codes.

### Pearson Edexcel

- **International GCSE** is graded 9–1 and is linear. Its components are weighted by the
  specification. Example: Physics 4PH1 is Paper 1, 110 marks at 61.1%, and Paper 2, 70 marks at
  38.9%.
- **"4 is a standard pass, 5 a strong pass"** is the UK Department for Education's wording for
  GCSEs. Pearson's own wording for International GCSE was not found.
- **International AS and A Level are modular.** Each unit gets a UMS (uniform mark scale) mark,
  and the units are combined ("cashed in") for the qualification.
  - A* needs an overall A and at least 90% of the A2 UMS.
  - On a resit, the best of the two most recent attempts counts.
  - Example: IAL Mathematics YMA01 is out of 600 UMS. A is 480, B 420, C 360, D 300 and E 240.
- **Entries** use a UCI, a 4-digit candidate number and entry codes, sent through Edexcel Online
  or A2C.

### International Baccalaureate

- **PYP.** No grades: teacher assessment and narrative reports. Schools report on the learner
  profile but must not grade it.
- **MYP.** Four criteria per subject, each 0–8, give a total out of 32. It converts to a grade
  as follows:

  | Criteria total | 0–5 | 6–9 | 10–14 | 15–18 | 19–23 | 24–27 | 28–32 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Grade | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |

  Criterion levels are the teacher's best-fit judgement, not an average. The MYP certificate
  needs at least 28 points and a grade 3 or higher in each eAssessment. The boundaries come from
  secondary sources quoting the IB; ibo.org blocked automated reading.
- **DP (verified on ibo.org).**
  - Six subjects graded 1–7, plus up to 3 core points, for a maximum of 45.
  - TOK and the Extended Essay are graded A–E. CAS is complete or not.
  - Core points matrix:

    | EE \ TOK | A | B | C | D | E |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | A | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | fail |
    | B | 3 | 2 | 2 | 1 | fail |
    | C | 2 | 2 | 1 | 0 | fail |
    | D | 2 | 1 | 0 | 0 | fail |
    | E | fail | fail | fail | fail | fail |

  - **All eight conditions must hold for the diploma:**
    1. CAS requirements are met.
    2. At least 24 points in total.
    3. A grade in every subject, TOK and the EE (no N).
    4. At least a 2 in every subject.
    5. No more than two grade 2s.
    6. No more than three grades of 3 or below.
    7. At least 12 points on HL subjects. With four HL subjects, the best three count.
    8. At least 9 points on SL subjects. With only two SL subjects, at least 5.

- **Predicted grades.** UCAS asks for "aspirational but achievable" predictions. International
  School Dhaka calls them "a best reasonable case, not a simple mean". **Record predicted,
  forecast and target grades separately, dated and approved; never calculate them from an
  average.**

## Bangladeshi context

- **Registration.** English-medium schools register under the Registration of Private Schools
  Ordinance 1962 and the Private School Registration Rules 2017. Many never have.
  - BANBEIS (the national education statistics bureau) reports about 148 English-medium schools,
    127 of them in Dhaka.
  - In June 2026 a minister announced a "common regulatory framework" for English-medium schools,
    without details.
  - Whether these schools get an EIIN (the government's school ID) was not verified.
- **VAT on tuition: unverified, and contested.** It was imposed and raised in 2007–2015, stayed
  by the High Court in 2015, and ruled not collectable from students by the Appellate Division in
  2017; the FY2017-18 budget still named it. The 5% rate quoted in the market review could not be
  confirmed. **Make VAT a per-fee-head setting that defaults to none**, and have a tax adviser
  confirm the rate.
- **Exams.** The British Council runs Cambridge and Pearson exams. Schools act as attached
  centres and register their students.
- **Data protection.** The Personal Data Protection Act 2026 applies:
  - verifiable guardian consent is needed for anyone under 18;
  - profiling of children is not allowed;
  - NID and passport numbers are sensitive data;
  - exports to Cambridge, Pearson and the IB should be logged.

## Software market

- **ManageBac is used by IB schools**, for example International School Dhaka and Aga Khan
  Academy Dhaka. **Toddle** is used for PYP at International School Dhaka. **OpenApply** handles
  admissions at several schools. None publish prices; they are quoted per student, likely
  thousands of US dollars a year (unverified).
- **Local ERPs** (Eduman, Edufy, Shoroborno, ClassTune) are built around national-curriculum GPA.
  - Edufy charges Tk 2,000–40,000 a month by tier, plus 5% VAT.
  - Shoroborno charges about Tk 6–7 per student per month.
- **Gaps a local product can win** (inferences from the evidence):
  1. Correct Cambridge and Pearson weighting, tiers and candidate exports. IB-first platforms and
     GPA-first local tools both miss this.
  2. IB criterion grading at a local price.
  3. bKash, Nagad and SSLCommerz, SMS, and invoices in BDT.
  4. A predicted-grade workflow.
  5. Bangla for guardians.
  6. The 2026 data law.
